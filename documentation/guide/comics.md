# Comics and manga

> **Level: 🟢 Basic** for adding a series, 🟡 Advanced for the library options.

Create a library of kind **Comics** or **Manga** (the adult kinds ask for a confirmation). Add a series from the dashboard: a manga page says volumes and chapters, a comics page says issues. Issues and volumes you have are matched from the file names, ComicInfo.xml and the folder; wanted ones are searched with the series title and the number.

- **Pull list**: the Calendar with weeks starting on Wednesday, for your comic libraries.
- **Quality**: pick a CBZ target (any page size, SD, HD, manga HD); a file whose pages are smaller than the target is an upgrade candidate.
- **Library options** (File Management): *Date type* (store or cover date), *A collected edition satisfies its issues*, and *Kavita and Komga files* (series.json and ComicInfo.xml, fill or replace).
- **Reading lists**: on the Story arcs page, *Import reading list* takes a ComicRack `.cbl` file, shows which books you hold and keeps those in order.

The model and the providers are in [comics.md](../comics.md).
